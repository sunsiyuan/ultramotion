An After Effects alternative, if you just need the video

After Effects is the standard tool for motion design, and learning it takes a while: keyframes, the graph editor, expressions, plugins. If what you need is a 20-second intro, a product launch clip or an explainer, there is a shorter route now. Describe the video to your AI coding agent and let it build it from a motion template.

How ultramotion works

Each template is an open-source Agent Skill for Claude Code, ChatGPT Codex and other agents that load skills. The scene is drawn in code, where every frame is a function of time, and a small Python script writes the music. Your agent fills the template with your content, renders it frame by frame to mp4 and adds the soundtrack. You end up with a finished 9:16 video, music included.

When to stay with After Effects

Client work that needs frame-exact control, compositing on top of live footage, character animation, or a team that already lives in AE templates. ultramotion covers a handful of styles well; it does not replace a motion designer.

Styles

Liquid Glass (Apple-style glass), Kinetic Type (bold type on the beat), Hand-drawn Explainer (whiteboard and chalkboard), Variety Captions (pop captions on your own footage) Light Particles (products, vehicles and places built from glowing particles), Flat Explainer (narrated, Kurzgesagt-style science explainers) Painterly (Van Gogh-style oil paintings that move), Ink Wash (Chinese ink painting that spreads and lives) and Event Replay (a moment in history replayed from real data). See them all on the styles page, each with the same prompt run with and without the skill.
