## gap
The Moon looks close.
At its farthest, the gap between us is almost four hundred thousand kilometres.

## fit
How big is that, really?
Let's try to fill it with planets.
Jupiter goes in first. Then Saturn, rings and all.
Uranus. Neptune.
Then the small ones: Mars, Venus, Mercury.

## left
Every planet in the solar system, lined up between us and the Moon.
And there's still a little room to spare.

## end
Space isn't empty because things are small.
It's empty because the distances are huge.
