# Collage News Explainer · 【剪报拼贴】一周新闻讲清楚

This week's ruling, policy or record, explained with the real documents, a highlighter and red string. (这周的判决、政策、纪录，用原件、剪报和红线讲清楚)

Engine: `lib/news-collage/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `meta-terms.html`
- `perseverance-record.html`
- `tariff-refunds.html`

Copy the starting file that matches the user's language (and the media it loads) into the project and keep its look, pacing, camera, type and music. Change only what the user's content changes:

- The story (in the example: a ruling, a policy or a record from this week)
- The document (in the example: the real page or clause)
- The numbers
- The headline, which is the answer
- Sources

Everything to change is in CONTENT at the top of the html and in data/*.json; the numbers come from the documents, with the sources on screen.

If the words are longer or shorter, change the size and the line breaks, not the structure. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
