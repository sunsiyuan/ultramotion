# Flat Animation Explainer · 【扁平动画】一分钟讲清一个数字

A narrated explainer: how big a surprising number really is. (带旁白的科普：一个惊人的数到底有多大)

Engine: `lib/flat-explainer/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `planets-gap/` — English
- `water-drop/` — Chinese

Copy the starting file that matches the user's language (and the media it loads) into the project and keep its look, pacing, camera, type and music. Change only what the user's content changes:

- The question (in the example: Could every planet fit between the Earth and the Moon?)
- The answer and what to compare it with
- The narration (or let the AI write it)

The scenes change with your question; the drawing, the camera, the pacing and the music stay the same.

If the words are longer or shorter, change the size and the line breaks, not the structure. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
