# Flat Animation Explainer · 【扁平动画】一分钟讲清一个数字

A narrated explainer: how big a surprising number really is. (带旁白的科普：一个惊人的数到底有多大)

Engine: `lib/flat-explainer/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `planets-gap/` — English
- `water-drop/` — Chinese

Read the starting page to see how it is made, then make the user's video on the same engine — copy what serves their content, change what doesn't. The camera, the motion, the pacing and the music follow what is in their content and what happens in it. What the user gives:

- The question (in the example: Could every planet fit between the Earth and the Moon?)
- The answer and what to compare it with
- The narration (or let the AI write it)

The scenes change with your question; the drawing, the camera, the pacing and the music stay the same.

Judge the result by the standard in SKILL.md (Judgment), not by how close it stays to the example. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
