# Cinematic Opening Credits · 【电影感】片头字幕

Opening titles for a documentary, a short film, a family film. (纪录片、短片、家庭影像的片头)

Engine: `lib/title-sequence/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `move-out-zh.html` — Chinese
- `the-last-keeper-en.html` — English
- `xiabancheng-zh.html` — Chinese

Read the starting page to see how it is made, then make the user's video on the same engine — copy what serves their content, change what doesn't. The camera, the motion, the pacing and the music follow what is in their content and what happens in it. What the user gives:

- Film title (in the example: The Last Keeper)
- What it is about (in the example: a documentary about the last lighthouse keeper on Lake Superior)
- Credits (in the example: Northshore Pictures presents · a film by Ana Ruiz · music by Tom Varga)

Judge the result by the standard in SKILL.md (Judgment), not by how close it stays to the example. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
