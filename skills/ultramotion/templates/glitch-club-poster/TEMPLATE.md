# Glitch Club Night Poster · 【故障风】电子音乐海报

A techno night, a club, a party. (电子音乐、夜店、派对活动)

Engine: `lib/glitch/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `concrete.html` — English
- `pendi.html` — Chinese

Read the starting page to see how it is made, then make the user's video on the same engine — copy what serves their content, change what doesn't. The camera, the motion, the pacing and the music follow what is in their content and what happens in it. What the user gives:

- The night (in the example: CONCRETE — Detroit techno all night)
- Date, venue, line-up (in the example: Sat Jan 16 · 10PM–6AM · The Foundry, Detroit · Mara Ellis / KRNL / Dex Aldana b2b Sol)
- Colours (optional) (in the example: black and white, one acid green)

Broken the whole way, breaking on the drums, with a moment for the name, the date and the venue to read clean.

Judge the result by the standard in SKILL.md (Judgment), not by how close it stays to the example. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
