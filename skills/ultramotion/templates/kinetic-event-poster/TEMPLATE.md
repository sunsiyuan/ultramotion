# Kinetic Typography Poster · 【动感排版】活动海报

A gig, a market, a pop-up, a talk night. (演出、市集、快闪、分享会)

Engine: `lib/kinetic-type/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `arcade-night-neon.html`
- `night-market-retro.html` — Chinese
- `type-coffee-swiss.html` — English

Read the starting page to see how it is made, then make the user's video on the same engine — copy what serves their content, change what doesn't. The camera, the motion, the pacing and the music follow what is in their content and what happens in it. What the user gives:

- The event (in the example: TYPE & COFFEE)
- Date and time (in the example: THU 7PM)
- Who or what is on
- One line at the end (in the example: BRING A FRIEND)

You can change the fonts and colours to suit your event; keep the beat and the way the words slam in.

Judge the result by the standard in SKILL.md (Judgment), not by how close it stays to the example. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
