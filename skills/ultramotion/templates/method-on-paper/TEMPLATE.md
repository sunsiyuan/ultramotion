# Hand-drawn Whiteboard Explainer · 【手绘】纸上讲方法

A method, a book note, a few steps. (一个方法、读书笔记、几个步骤)

Engine: `lib/whiteboard/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `habit-loop-colorful.html`
- `leap-year-notebook.html` — English
- `pomodoro-whiteboard.html` — Chinese

Copy the starting file that matches the user's language (and the media it loads) into the project and keep its look, pacing, camera, type and music. Change only what the user's content changes:

- The tip or fact (in the example: Why we have leap years)
- Three or four steps

The notes change with your topic; the paper, the pen, the camera and the pacing stay the same.

If the words are longer or shorter, change the size and the line breaks, not the structure. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
