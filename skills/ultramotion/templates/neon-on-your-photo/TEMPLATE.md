# Neon Sign on Your Shopfront Photo · 【霓虹】照片上的招牌

Your own shopfront photo, with the sign switching on over it. (用你自己门面的照片，招牌在上面通电亮起)

Engine: `lib/neon-sign/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `fade-and-co.html` — English
- `gebi-bar.html` — Chinese

Read the starting page to see how it is made, then make the user's video on the same engine — copy what serves their content, change what doesn't. The camera, the motion, the pacing and the music follow what is in their content and what happens in it. What the user gives:

- A photo of your shopfront (in the example: a photo of the shop at dusk, upright)
- What the sign says (in the example: FADE & CO. · BARBERS — a neighborhood barbershop in Bushwick, Brooklyn)
- Where on the photo it hangs (in the example: the blank dark panel above the door and the window)

Judge the result by the standard in SKILL.md (Judgment), not by how close it stays to the example. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
