# Pixel Art Birthday Level Up · 【像素风】宠物升级生日卡

A birthday or anniversary card from your own photo — a pet, a kid. (用你自己的照片做生日、纪念日祝福，宠物和孩子都合适)

Engine: `lib/pixel/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `doubao-birthday.html` — Chinese

Copy the starting file that matches the user's language (and the media it loads) into the project and keep its look, pacing, camera, type and music. Change only what the user's content changes:

- Photo (in the example: pet.jpg (plain background, face in the middle))
- Name
- Age, from → to (in the example: 13 → 14)
- The stats card

Everything to change is in CONTENT and COLORS at the top of the html.

If the words are longer or shorter, change the size and the line breaks, not the structure. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
