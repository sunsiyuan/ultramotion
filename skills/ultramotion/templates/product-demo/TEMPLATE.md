# Product Demo Video · 【真实界面】产品演示

A launch or a new feature: your real app in a phone or a browser, clicked, typed into, zoomed in on. (新功能上线、产品发布、App 测评：真界面在手机或浏览器里被用起来)

Engine: `lib/ui-demo/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `excalidraw-browser-en.html` — English
- `open-meteo-vertical-en.html` — English
- `squoosh-phone-en.html` — English

Read the starting page to see how it is made, then make the user's video on the same engine — copy what serves their content, change what doesn't. The camera, the motion, the pacing and the music follow what is in their content and what happens in it. What the user gives:

- Your product (in the example: its URL, or your screenshots or screen recording)
- What to do in it (in the example: click, type, drag…)
- Captions (in the example: three to five short lines)
- The payoff number (in the example: read from the capture)
- Phone or browser

Capture the real screens with lib/ui-demo/capture.py; the steps and captions are at the top of the html.

Judge the result by the standard in SKILL.md (Judgment), not by how close it stays to the example. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
