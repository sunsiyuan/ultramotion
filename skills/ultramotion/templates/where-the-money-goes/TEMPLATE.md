# Where the Money Goes Infographic · 【信息图】一块钱去了哪

An income, a budget or a bill split by what it pays for. (收入、预算、开销按用途拆开)

Engine: `lib/infographic/` — read its GUIDE.md.

## Start from

- `china-wallet.html` — Chinese
- `us-food-dollar.html` — English

Read the starting page to see how it is made, then make the user's video on the same engine — copy what serves their content, change what doesn't. The camera, the motion, the pacing and the music follow what is in their content and what happens in it. What the user gives:

- Which money (in the example: a US food dollar in 2023)
- Split by (in the example: the industries it pays (farms, processing, packaging, transport, stores, restaurants …))
- Data source (in the example: USDA ERS Food Dollar Series)

Use real data and compute every number on screen from the data file.

Judge the result by the standard in SKILL.md (Judgment), not by how close it stays to the example. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
