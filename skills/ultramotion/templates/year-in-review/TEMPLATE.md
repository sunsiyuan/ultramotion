# Year in Review Video · 【相册】年度回顾

Your year: twelve photos, a few numbers, one line to end on. (自己的一年：十二张照片、几个数字、一句话收尾)

Engine: none — the starting page draws everything itself (Canvas 2D); render it with `render.py`.

## Start from

- `my-2026.html`

Copy the starting file that matches the user's language (and the media it loads) into the project and keep its look, pacing, camera, type and music. Change only what the user's content changes:

- Whose year
- Twelve photos, one a month (in the example: your own photos, vertical works best)
- One line per photo
- Three numbers from the year (in the example: 1 very good boy, 9 cities, 412 miles run)
- The last line (in the example: thank you, 2026.)

Everything to change is in CONTENT at the top of the html; put the photos in photos/.

If the words are longer or shorter, change the size and the line breaks, not the structure. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
