# Year in Review Video · 【相册】年度回顾

Your year: twelve photos, a few numbers, one line to end on. (自己的一年：十二张照片、几个数字、一句话收尾)

Engine: none — the starting page draws everything itself (Canvas 2D); render it with `render.py`.

## Start from

- `my-2026.html`

Read the starting page to see how it is made, then make the user's video on the same engine — copy what serves their content, change what doesn't. The camera, the motion, the pacing and the music follow what is in their content and what happens in it. What the user gives:

- Whose year
- Twelve photos, one a month (in the example: your own photos, vertical works best)
- One line per photo
- Three numbers from the year (in the example: 1 very good boy, 9 cities, 412 miles run)
- The last line (in the example: thank you, 2026.)

Everything to change is in CONTENT at the top of the html; put the photos in photos/.

Judge the result by the standard in SKILL.md (Judgment), not by how close it stays to the example. Write the content for the user's place and audience; don't translate the example's.
