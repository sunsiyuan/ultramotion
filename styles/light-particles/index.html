Light Particles

Hundreds of thousands of glowing particles that gather into products, vehicles, towns and titles, build up in order and light up — for reveals, launches and greetings.

Use it

Install the skills, then ask your agent for a video in this style. For example:

npx skills add sunsiyuan/ultramotion
View the prompt

Make a vertical light-particle motion video (thousands of glowing particles that gather into objects and text, scatter and re-form, with glow). Content: a launch teaser for Echo One, a new pair of wireless earbuds — available March 3. Requirements: - Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 15–25 seconds - Music and sound effects; export one mp4 with audio to the current directory
