Liquid Glass

Apple-style glass that pops, stretches, merges and bends the text behind it, on a soft gradient, cut to a beat.

Use it

Install the skills, then ask your agent for a video in this style. For example:

npx skills add sunsiyuan/ultramotion
View the prompt

Make a launch video for a new product feature, in the Liquid Glass style (the glassy look of Apple's Liquid Glass). Product: ultramotion, a library of motion templates. New feature: Liquid Glass templates — turn one sentence into a vertical motion video with music. Requirements: - Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 15–25 seconds - Structure: open on the product name → show the new feature in 2–3 scenes → end with a tagline and the product name - Include music and sound effects; export one mp4 with audio to the current directory
