Variety Captions

Variety-show captions on your own footage: pop words, bursts, stamps, subtitles and sound effects, placed around the face.

Use it

Install the skills, then ask your agent for a video in this style. For example:

npx skills add sunsiyuan/ultramotion
View the prompt

Add variety-show captions to talk.mp4 in the current directory — the bold, outlined, pop-in words and stickers you see on reality and variety shows. Requirements: - Emphasise the key lines and the reactions with captions, with sound effects - Keep the original audio - Export one mp4 with audio to the current directory
