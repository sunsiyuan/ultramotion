Hand-drawn Explainer

Whiteboard, notebook or chalkboard explainers, written stroke by stroke as the camera moves across the board, with its own music.

Use it

Install the skills, then ask your agent for a video in this style. For example:

npx skills add sunsiyuan/ultramotion

Make a vertical hand-drawn whiteboard explainer video (lines drawn stroke by stroke, handwritten text, arrows and little doodle icons, like someone explaining while drawing on a whiteboard). Content: the 50/30/20 budget rule — half of your take-home pay goes to needs, 30% to wants, 20% to savings, and why it keeps you on track. Requirements: - Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 15–25 seconds - Music and sound effects; export one mp4 with audio to the current directory
